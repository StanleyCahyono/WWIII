Legend

Game-only overlays (deliberate, outside the standard frame)

  • Selection ring — yellow ring with a dark outline drawn around the symbol. Never changes the identity frame, fill or icon.
  • Age badge — dashed white pill at top-right with the observation age (<1h, Nh, Nd) for stale/historical reports.
  • Muted styling — stale reports are drawn at reduced opacity. Geometry is unchanged, so it stays distinguishable from both selection (ring) and disabled UI.
  • Standard amplifiers drawn by the renderer (not game overlays): planned = dashed frame (status digit 1); damaged = operational-condition bar (status 3); HQ staff (HQ digit 2, officers only); echelon above the frame (land units, verified echelon only — never inferred from stack count); unique designation and higher formation beside the frame.
  • Unidentified contacts use a symbol-set frame with no entity icon, no modifiers, no echelon and no HQ, so no hidden branch is encoded.

Every supported entity under four affiliations (present)

Expected frames: friendly rectangle/rounded frame, blue; hostile diamond/pointed frame, red; neutral square frame, green; unknown quatrefoil/cloverleaf frame, yellow. Air, surface, subsurface, equipment and installation symbols use the edition's domain-specific variants of those frames.
